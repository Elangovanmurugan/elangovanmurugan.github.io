MSc Dissertation · Ongoing · Graph Theory

Mapping the North–South divide in the UK rail network

Built a nationwide weighted rail network of 2,588 stations and 3,658 connections in NetworkX, then tested four different definitions of the UK's North–South boundary against it using graph-theoretic measures and Random Forest / SVM models. The geographic boundary definition separated the network with a 100% F1-score — a data-backed way to talk about regional transport inequality.
